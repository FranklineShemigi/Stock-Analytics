# NSE Stock Analytics

A web-based historical market analytics platform for the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE).

The project allows users to explore NSE-listed companies, sectors, historical prices, performance and market data using historical records dating back to 2007.

## 🚀 Features

- 📊 NSE market dashboard
- 🏢 Sector explorer
- 🏦 Individual company analytics
- 📈 Historical share-price charts
- 📉 Trading-volume charts
- 📅 Historical data from 2007 onwards
- 🔍 Company search
- ⚖️ Company performance comparison
- ⭐ Company watchlist
- 📥 CSV export
- 📱 Progressive Web App (PWA)
- 📊 Performance rankings
- 📆 Custom historical date ranges

## 📈 Historical Analysis

Explore company performance across different periods:

- 1 Week
- 1 Month
- 3 Months
- 6 Months
- 1 Year
- 5 Years
- Custom date ranges
- Maximum available historical period

The goal is to make long-term NSE market data easier to explore, compare and understand.

## 🏢 Sector Explorer

Explore NSE companies by sector.

Selecting a sector displays the companies within that sector and allows users to continue to individual company analytics.

## 📊 Company Analytics

Each company has its own analytics page containing information such as:

- Latest available price
- Price performance
- Historical price chart
- Trading volume
- Previous close
- Day high
- Day low
- Sector
- Market performance ranking

More advanced analytics will continue to be added as the project develops.

## 📱 Progressive Web App

NSE Stock Analytics is being developed as a Progressive Web App.

Supported browsers can install the application on compatible devices and use it more like a native application.


## 📚 Data
Historical NSE market data is organized by year.
The yearly price files are loaded by the application and normalized before being used for analytics and visualization.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer
This project is intended primarily as a portfolio, educational and analytical project.
Historical market data may contain missing records, corporate actions, ticker changes or other limitations.
NSE Stock Analytics is not financial advice and should not be used as a substitute for official market information.
